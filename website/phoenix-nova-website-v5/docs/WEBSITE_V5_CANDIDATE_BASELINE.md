# Phoenix Nova website V5 Baseline

Status: ACTIVE CANDIDATE / PRIVATE REVIEW  
Date: 2026-09-02  
Production impact: NONE

## 1. Version boundary

- V4.0 / V4.1 remain the stable reference and are not overwritten, deleted, or rolled back.
- V5 is an independent candidate for narrative, information-architecture, and visual validation.
- Candidate routes remain `noindex, nofollow` until a separate Production Gate is approved.
- `/academy`, `/blueprint`, and `/family-portal` production aliases are not introduced in this candidate.

## 2. V5 thesis

V5 is a premium brand portal rather than a product catalogue. It leads with:

> 一个入口，走进凤启世界。

The first screen establishes Phoenix Nova™ as a Global Family Growth Platform and immediately exposes four primary paths. A curated portal directory then connects the complete family-growth platform and the distinct brand worlds without turning the experience into a dense SaaS dashboard.

## 3. Preserved foundations

- Phoenix Nova™ Official Logo System V1.0 only.
- Phoenix Navy `#0D1B2A`, Phoenix Gold `#C8A24A`, and Ivory White.
- Global Family Growth Platform.
- For Every Beginning. / 每一次启程，都值得更好的未来。
- Primary journey: Homepage → Phoenix Compass™ → Growth Blueprint → Phoenix Family OS™.
- Three public Compass entries: Child Compass™, Education Compass™, Family Compass™.
- Six family journeys: Next Generation, Hong Kong Pathway, Family Protection, Business Growth, Global Planning, Family Legacy.
- Five supporting capabilities: Education, Identity, Wealth, Health, Global Living.
- Chinese master copy with equivalent English routes.
- Selected approved V4 assets and product components are reused rather than redrawn.

## 4. V5 structural decisions

- The homepage starts as a clear brand gateway with visible destinations in the first viewport.
- Compass remains the core family-growth entrance and is the lead portal tile.
- Growth Lighthouse is a website narrative label for the bridge to Growth Blueprint; it is not a new product.
- Family OS is shown as the long-term continuity space, within current MVP boundaries.
- Phoenix Oriental is a distinct cultural-derivative world. The main site provides a restrained doorway and does not mix it into education or family services.
- 数字凤启 / Phoenix Nova Digital World is a distinct external doorway. Its homepage tile reuses the approved immortal-guardian image and links directly to the existing private Digital World Site.
- 《维港之上》 sits within Phoenix Insights as serial fiction. It is not a homepage product and not a direct wealth-services advertisement.
- Journeys and capabilities are shown as different concepts: journeys describe family needs; capabilities support them.

## 5. Explicit exclusions

- No WisWealth or KAIDE content.
- No unsupported backend, full CRM, production authentication, payment, or live Compass claims.
- No admission, immigration, medical, legal, or return guarantees.
- No “20 years” corporate-history claim in public copy.
- No “Root · Wing · World / 根·翼·世界” slogan.
- No redrawn, AI-generated, distorted, or approximate Phoenix Nova™ logo.

## 6. Candidate routes

- `/zh` and `/en`
- `/{locale}/compass`
- `/{locale}/lighthouse`
- `/{locale}/services`
- `/{locale}/insights`
- `/{locale}/oriental`
- `/{locale}/about`
- `/{locale}/family-center`

The `数字凤启 / Digital World` navigation item is an external link to the existing owner-only Digital World Site; it does not introduce a duplicate local route.

Locale switching preserves the equivalent route.

## 7. Acceptance gate

- Official logo and brand palette are used accurately.
- Chinese and English routes carry equivalent meaning.
- All navigation destinations work on desktop and mobile.
- The first screen communicates brand, audience, and the four primary portal paths.
- The homepage portal exposes all seven destinations without presenting them as disconnected products.
- 数字凤启 uses the approved immortal-guardian artwork and opens the existing Digital World Site.
- Phoenix Oriental and 《维港之上》 appear only in their approved positions.
- Family OS presentation stays within MVP scope.
- Candidate is owner-only and does not replace the V4 production site.
- Production build passes before private deployment.
