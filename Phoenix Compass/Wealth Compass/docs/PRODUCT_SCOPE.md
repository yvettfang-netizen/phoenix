# Product Scope

Wealth Compass™ V0.1 is an engineering foundation for a family wealth health and long-term planning entry experience. It may frame education funding, family risk protection, cash-flow health, long-term goals, and areas of family attention.

It does not recommend financial products, promise returns or application outcomes, create urgency, process D3 sensitive data, call OpenAI APIs, or connect to production systems. Automated reports require approved rules plus fixed templates.
